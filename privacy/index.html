And Omega Studio Tools

And Omega Studio Tools is an internal app used only by the And Omega Studios team to manage our own YouTube channel. With our permission, it uses Google APIs to:

  • read statistics, analytics, and comments for the And Omega Studios channel,
  • reply to comments and update thumbnails on our own videos,
  • send notification emails from our account to our own team, and
  • create and update documents it made in our Google Drive.

Public comments left on our videos are read only to notify our team and help us respond. We do not sell, rent, or share this information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Access tokens are stored on our own computer and are never published. Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account permissions page.

And Omega Studio Tools' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
